About ARK Embroidery Services

Custom embroidery Colorado Springs — ARK Screen Printing offers professional embroidered apparel that won't fade, crack, or peel. A premium, three-dimensional look for corporate apparel, team uniforms, and promotional products.

Whether you're outfitting your sales team with embroidered polos, creating custom hats for a promotional campaign, or producing uniforms for Fort Carson personnel, we deliver exceptional results tailored to Colorado Springs' unique market.

Why Choose Custom Embroidery?

Embroidery uses needle and thread to create your design directly in the fabric. It's durable, it's beautiful, and it works on just about any material. If it can be hooped, we can embroider it!

Unlike screen printing that sits on top of the fabric, embroidery becomes part of the garment. No fading. No cracking. No peeling. Just a professional, textured finish that looks great wash after wash.

We use only the best materials and techniques to bring your designs to life. Why? Because we care about quality. We care about durability. And we want you to wear your custom embroidery with pride for years to come.

Whether you need corporate polos for your team, custom hats for a brewery promotion, or uniforms that can handle Colorado's weather extremes, embroidery delivers the premium look and lasting durability your brand deserves.

Common Questions

What's the minimum order for custom embroidery?

ARK Screen Printing has no minimum order on custom embroidery. Order as few or as many items as you need.

How long does custom embroidery take?

Standard turnaround time is 7-14 business days from artwork approval. Rush services are available for time-sensitive projects.

What file formats do you need for embroidery?

We can work with most file formats including AI, EPS, PDF, and high-resolution PNG or JPG files. Digitization is included at no additional cost.
