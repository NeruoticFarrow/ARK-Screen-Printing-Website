Custom Apparel for Palmer Lake's Historic Tri-Lakes Community

Palmer Lake is a small, historic town of about 3,000 residents perched on the Palmer Divide at 7,200 feet — the high point between Denver and Colorado Springs on the Tri-Lakes corridor. Known for sweeping Rocky Mountain views, a charming downtown with independent shops and restaurants, and an active arts scene anchored by the Tri-Lakes Center for the Arts, Palmer Lake punches well above its size for community character. Custom apparel for gallery shows, community festivals, trail races, and local business staff is a natural fit.

Palmer Lake is part of the Lewis-Palmer School District 38, so students attend Lewis-Palmer High School Rangers and Palmer Ridge High School Bears — the same programs serving the broader Tri-Lakes community. The area's strong trail system and proximity to Monument Lake and the Santa Fe Trail generate outdoor recreation traffic and events throughout the year. We're about 25 miles south in Colorado Springs and regularly serve the entire Tri-Lakes corridor.

One shirt or a thousand — no minimum.

Screen printing needs a screen made for each ink colour, so it pays off as the quantity grows. DTF needs no screens, so it's the better buy for a handful of shirts or full-colour art. Tell us your quantity and we'll recommend the cheaper method — you don't need to know which one you want.
