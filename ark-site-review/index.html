One shirt or a thousand — no minimum.

Screen printing needs a screen made for each ink colour, so it starts at 24 shirts — that's where it pays off. Under 24, we print with DTF: no screens, no setup, full colour, even for a single shirt. Tell us your quantity and we'll recommend the right method — you don't need to know which one you want.

How an order works.

Price in minutes, proof right after, printing the same day — and nothing prints until you approve it.

  1. 01

    See your price instantly

    Pick the garment, the quantity and where it's printed — the form shows your price as you go. About two minutes, or call or text and talk it through.

  2. 02

    We're in touch within minutes

    We call or text within ten minutes (7:30am–7pm Mountain Time, every day) — or email if we miss you — to confirm sizes, artwork and timing.

  3. 03

    Your proof, moments later

    A digital proof of your design on the garment follows right after we talk. Nothing is printed until you approve it.

  4. 04

    Printing starts the same day

    Approve the proof and pay the invoice, and printing can start the same day — most screen-print orders are done in 5–7 business days. Local pickup available, or we ship anywhere in the US.

Straight answers.

All questions
Is there a minimum order?

No. Screen printing starts at 24 shirts; under 24 we print with DTF, which needs no screens — even a single shirt. Tell us your quantity and we'll recommend the right method.

How long does an order take?

Most screen-print orders are done in 5–7 business days, DTF in 3–5 business days from artwork approval and embroidery in 7–14 business days. Need it for a date? Put the date in your quote request or call — rush is often possible.

Will I see my design before you print it?

Yes. A digital proof — a mockup of your design on the garment — follows moments after we've talked. Nothing is printed until you approve it.

Can you help with my artwork?

Yes. Send a logo file, a sketch or just an idea and we'll prepare it for print. Our artwork guidelines explain which files work best.

Do you do embroidery?

Yes — polos, hats, jackets and bags, with free digitizing on every order and no minimum order. See custom embroidery.

Is local pickup available?

Yes — local pickup available in Colorado Springs, or we'll ship your order anywhere in the US.

How fast is it?

Your price shows as you fill in the form. We're in touch within ten minutes (7:30am–7pm Mountain Time, every day), your proof follows moments later, and once you approve it, printing can start the same day.
