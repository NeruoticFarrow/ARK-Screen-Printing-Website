Straight answers.

Creator FAQ
Does it cost anything to open a store?

No. A store is $0 to open, with no monthly fee and no listing fee. Each sale has a base — the blank, the print, packing and our margin — and everything you charge above it is yours.

Who prints and ships the orders?

We do, in our Colorado Springs shop. Print-on-demand orders ship within 3–5 business days, with tracking (carrier time is extra). Store-order shipping is $7.99 flat and free over $75, US addresses only.

How do I get paid?

Earnings become available 21 days after an order ships and are paid by PayPal once a month when you have $20 or more. A W-9 is needed once payouts pass the IRS reporting threshold.

Can I sell a limited run instead?

Yes. Run a campaign with a closing date and we print the whole run together. 24+ orders in a run are screen printed when the art fits the colour limits (up to 2 colours from 24, up to 6 from 40); otherwise they're DTF printed. Campaign orders print after the closing date, within 5 business days of the blanks arriving, then ship. Every order prints either way.

What do my buyers get?

Tracking on every order, free size exchanges within 30 days and defects made right within 30 days of delivery.

Would a bulk order suit me better?

If you'd rather buy upfront — for an event, a team or staff — a bulk order gives the lowest price per shirt (screen printing from 24). Get an Instant Quote.
