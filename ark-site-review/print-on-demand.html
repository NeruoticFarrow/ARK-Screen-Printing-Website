Every order prints. From 24, it's screen printed by hand.

24+ orders in a run are screen printed when the art fits the colour limits (up to 2 colours from 24, up to 6 from 40); otherwise they're DTF printed. Campaign orders print after the closing date, within 5 business days of the blanks arriving, then ship. What you keep is the same either way.

Straight answers.

Does it cost anything to open a store?

No. A store is $0 to open, with no monthly fee and no listing fee. Each sale has a base — the blank, the print, packing and our margin — and everything you charge above it is yours.

How much is shipping?

Your buyers pay it: $8 for the first item and $3 for each additional item, up to $35 an order, to US addresses. Every order ships with tracking within 3–5 business days (carrier time is extra).

Who prints and ships the orders?

We do, in our Colorado Springs shop — every order is printed, packed and shipped here, not sent to a warehouse. You can call or text the shop.

What can I put my designs on?

Every category in our designer: short and long sleeves, hoodies, sweatshirts, tote bags, polos, youth sizes, overalls and more. See the categories.

How do I get paid?

Earnings become available 21 days after an order ships and are paid by PayPal once a month when you have $20 or more. A W-9 is needed once payouts pass the IRS reporting threshold.

Can I sell a limited run instead?

Yes. Run a campaign with a closing date and we print the whole run together. 24+ orders in a run are screen printed when the art fits the colour limits (up to 2 colours from 24, up to 6 from 40); otherwise they're DTF printed. Campaign orders print after the closing date, within 5 business days of the blanks arriving, then ship. Every order prints either way.

What do my buyers get?

Tracking on every order, free size exchanges within 30 days and defects made right within 30 days of delivery.

Can I open a store today?

Stores are invite-only while we finish testing. Request an invite by email and we'll set you up.

Would a bulk order suit me better?

If you'd rather buy upfront — for an event, a team or staff — a bulk order gives the lowest price per shirt (screen printing from 24). Price a bulk order.
