Why ARK?

ARK Screen Printing is a Colorado Springs shop that does everything in-house — screen printing, DTF printing, and embroidery all under one roof. That means faster turnaround, better quality control, and one point of contact for your entire order.

We print from 1 shirt to 1,000. Small orders use DTF so there's no minimum and no wasted setup cost. Larger orders use screen printing for maximum value. Need printed shirts and embroidered hats for the same event? We handle both.

We also ship nationwide — so if you're not local to Colorado Springs, you can still get ARK quality delivered to your door.
