Give the production plan enough context

What to send in a Fountain custom-apparel request

The correct print path depends on how the garments will be used, the artwork, the garment choice, and the amount of production required.

  • Artwork or logo and desired garment use
  • Garment type, color, and any durability needs
  • Quantity, size range, and possible reorder needs
  • Front, back, sleeve, or multiple placement details
  • Needed-by date and fulfillment notes

Quote-first workflow
