Custom apparel for an Old Colorado City idea or program

Old Colorado City has a distinctly historic, independent-business character that suits a wide range of custom-apparel requests: shop merchandise, hospitality and service staff pieces, creative-event garments, and small-group orders. The best starting point is to define how the apparel will be seen, used, and reordered.

ARK serves Old Colorado City from Colorado Springs. The quote form is built for the details that change production decisions: artwork, garment, coverage, quantity, colors, print locations, and the calendar behind the project.

Start with the intended experience

What makes an Old Colorado City quote specific

The most compelling custom pieces keep the brand or event idea connected to the garment, print method, and day-to-day use.

  • Artwork reference and intended visual feel
  • Garment preference and colorway
  • First-run count, sizes, and future reorder outlook
  • Print coverage and placement requirements
  • Launch, event, or handoff timeline

Quote-first workflow

How an Old Colorado City apparel idea becomes a production plan

  1. Share the concept, artwork, and known production details.
  2. ARK reviews the garment and print-method options that fit the intended use.
  3. The quote, art prep, and proofing path are set before the run is scheduled.
  4. After approval, ARK produces the garments in Colorado Springs and confirms fulfillment details.
