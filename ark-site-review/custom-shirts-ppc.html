Before you ask.

What's the minimum for custom t-shirts?

There isn't one. Screen printing starts at 24 shirts; smaller orders — even a single piece — are printed with DTF, which needs no screens and no setup.

How long do custom shirts take?

Most screen-print orders are done in 5–7 business days. DTF orders take 3–5 business days from artwork approval, and embroidery 7–14 business days. Need it sooner? Call or text before you order — rush is often possible.

Can you help with my design?

Yes. Send whatever you have — a logo file, a sketch or an idea — and we'll prepare it for print. Our artwork guidelines explain which files print best.

Is local pickup available?

Yes — local pickup available in Colorado Springs, or we'll ship it to you anywhere in the US.

Get your quote.

Tell us the garment, the quantity, your artwork and your date. Your price shows as you go. We reply within ten minutes (7:30am–7pm Mountain Time, every day), your proof follows moments later, and printing can start the same day.
