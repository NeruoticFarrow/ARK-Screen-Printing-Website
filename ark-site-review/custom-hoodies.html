One shirt or a thousand — no minimum.

Screen printing needs a screen made for each ink colour, so it starts at 24 shirts — that's where it pays off. Under 24, we print with DTF: no screens, no setup, full colour, even for a single shirt. Straight answers.

All questions
Is there a minimum for custom hoodies?

There isn't one. Screen printing starts at 24 shirts; smaller orders — even a single piece — are printed with DTF, which needs no screens and no setup.

Should my hoodie be printed or embroidered?

Printing suits larger designs and back prints; embroidery suits a small chest logo and looks stitched and premium. Tell us the look you want and we'll recommend one — or show you both on the proof.

How long do custom hoodies take?

Most screen-print orders are done in 5–7 business days. DTF orders take 3–5 business days from artwork approval, and embroidery 7–14 business days. Need it sooner? Call or text before you order — rush is often possible.

Is local pickup available?

Yes — local pickup available in Colorado Springs, or we'll ship it to you anywhere in the US.
