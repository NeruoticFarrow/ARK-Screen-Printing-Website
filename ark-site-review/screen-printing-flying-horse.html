A considered Flying Horse apparel plan

Custom garments that feel intentional from first run to reorder

A Flying Horse custom-apparel request may be a polished business program, a community gathering, a professional event kit, or a branded merchandise concept. These are not interchangeable jobs, so the quote should establish the garment feel, the visual standard, and the production requirements before the order is built.

ARK serves Flying Horse from Colorado Springs. Tell us what the garments need to accomplish and include the artwork, quantity, print locations, colors, and deadline. That context makes it easier to recommend the right production route.

Production choices that support the presentation

Three Flying Horse apparel programs to define early

The page describes project types only; it does not state or imply affiliation with any Flying Horse organization, club, venue, or developer.

01 · PROGRAM

Staff and professional apparel

For a polished team program, identify the logo hierarchy, role differences, garment standards, and future reorder requirements before the first run is quoted.

02 · OCCASION

Gathering and event pieces

Event apparel works best when the guest count, size range, print locations, timeline, and distribution approach are known at the start.

03 · PRODUCT

Premium merchandise concepts

For garments intended to sell or represent a premium experience, use the quote to discuss fabric, hand feel, print coverage, and the intended retail impression.

Make the presentation measurable

The details that move a Flying Horse quote forward

A strong initial brief helps protect both the visual quality and the operational side of a custom-garment program.

  • Brand artwork and any approved color references
  • Garment category, fit, and preferred fabric feel
  • Approximate quantity and size assortment
  • Placement needs for front, back, sleeve, or personalization
  • Launch or event date and any packaging/distribution notes

Quote-first workflow

How a Flying Horse apparel concept becomes an order

  1. Describe the garment program and submit the available artwork.
  2. ARK evaluates the scope, garment direction, print methods, and timing.
  3. The quote and approval path are set before production is scheduled.
  4. After approval, ARK prints the project in Colorado Springs and confirms fulfillment details.

Choose the finish after the brief

Treat garment and print method as one decision

The best visual result comes from choosing the garment and print treatment together. A retail-forward shirt, a uniform piece, and a once-a-year event tee may call for different screen printing, DTF, embroidery, water-based, or discharge choices.

Questions before you quote?

Custom Screen Printing Flying Horse FAQ

Short answers to the decisions that make a custom-apparel request easier to scope.

Can ARK help plan premium custom merchandise for Flying Horse?

Yes. Include the intended garment feel, artwork, print coverage, initial quantity, and presentation goals in the quote request.

Can I request staff apparel and merchandise in the same quote?

Yes. Separate the programs by garment, artwork, and quantity so the production approach for each can be evaluated clearly.

What is useful to include when I have a fixed event date?

Share the in-hands date, expected count, artwork status, garment direction, and any distribution plan as early as possible.

Does this page represent a Flying Horse storefront?

No. ARK is based in Colorado Springs and serves Flying Horse projects through the quote process.
