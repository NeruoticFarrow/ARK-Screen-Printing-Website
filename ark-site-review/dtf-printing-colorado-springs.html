What Is DTF Printing?

Direct to Film printing is a heat-transfer process that works like this: your design is printed in full color onto a special PET film, coated with a hot-melt adhesive powder, and then heat-pressed onto your garment. The result is a smooth, flexible print that bonds directly with the fabric fibers.

Unlike traditional screen printing, DTF requires no screens and no color separation — which means there's no minimum order quantity to make it cost-effective. You get the same quality result whether you're printing 1 shirt or 50.

Unlike vinyl heat transfer, DTF handles complex artwork, gradients, and photographic images without any cutting or weeding. If your design can be printed, DTF can transfer it.

DTF vs. Screen Printing: When We Use Each

At ARK, we use the right method for your order size so you always get the best value:

  • 1–5 shirts → DTF printing. No setup cost makes DTF the clear choice for small orders.
  • 6+ shirts → screen printing. At higher quantities, screen printing's per-shirt cost drops significantly and delivers exceptional durability.

You don't have to figure this out yourself — just tell us your quantity and we'll recommend the right method and give you a quote.

What We Can Print On

Common Questions About DTF Printing

What is the minimum order for DTF printing?

There is no minimum. We print as few as 1 shirt. DTF is specifically designed to be cost-effective at any quantity.

How long does DTF printing take?

Standard turnaround is 3–5 business days from artwork approval. Rush services are available — contact us to discuss your timeline.

What file formats do you need?

We prefer vector files (AI, EPS, PDF) for best results, but we can work with high-resolution PNG or JPG files as well. When in doubt, send us what you have and we'll let you know.
