The production conversation

A soft-hand print starts with the right print plan

Water-based ink is often requested when a brand, event, or merch program wants a lower-build print that feels more integrated with the garment. It is not simply a softer version of every screen-print job. The fabric, garment color, art coverage, and desired opacity all affect whether it is the right route.

At ARK, a water-based request begins as a production conversation. Share the artwork, garment direction, quantity, and print locations through the quote form. We will help determine whether water-based ink supports the finish you want, or whether another in-house method is the more dependable choice for the project.

Questions before you quote?

Water Based Screen Printing Services FAQ

Clear expectations make specialty screen printing work better. These answers cover the conversations we use to scope a project.

Can I request water-based ink for a custom shirt order?

Yes. Use the quote form and note that you are interested in a water-based finish. Include the garment, artwork, quantity, and print locations so the recommendation can be tied to the actual job.

Does water-based screen printing work on every garment?

No printing method is the right answer for every fabric, color, or artwork. Garment composition, dye, artwork coverage, and the expected look all matter, which is why ARK reviews specialty-ink requests before production.

Will a water-based print always be the softest option?

The final feel depends on the garment and the design as well as the ink. If the requested finish or opacity needs a different approach, we will explain that before a job moves forward.

Can you help choose garments for a water-based project?

Yes. Tell us what you plan to print—or what you want the finished shirt to feel like—and include that in the quote request. We can discuss a garment direction as part of the production plan.

Do you ship water-based screen printed apparel outside Colorado?

Yes. ARK prints in Colorado Springs and ships custom apparel orders nationwide.

How do I start a water-based screen printing project?

Use the quote form below. Upload or describe the artwork, tell us the quantity and garment direction, and note that water-based ink is part of the request.
