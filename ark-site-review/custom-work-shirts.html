Before you ask.

What's the minimum for custom t-shirts?

Yes. Our minimum is 12 garments per order. For 12–23 garments, we may recommend DTF; screen printing starts at 24 shirts.

How long do custom shirts take?

Most screen-print orders are done in 5–10 business days. DTF orders take 3–5 business days from artwork approval, and embroidery 7–14 business days. Need it sooner? Call or text before you order — rush is often possible.

Can you help with my design?

Yes — free. Send whatever you have — a logo file, a sketch, a photo or an idea — and we'll clean it up for print at no charge. Our artwork guidelines explain which files print best.

Is local pickup available?

Yes — local pickup available in Colorado Springs, or we'll ship it to you anywhere in the US.
