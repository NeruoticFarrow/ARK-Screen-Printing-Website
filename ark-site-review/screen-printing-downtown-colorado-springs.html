Three Downtown project paths worth planning for

These are practical planning paths, not claims about ARK clients or affiliations in Downtown.

01 · MERCH

Retail and brand merchandise

Share the garment feel, colorway, design coverage, and first-run quantity. We can help match the print approach to the way you want the product to wear and represent the brand.

02 · EVENT

Events, volunteers, and teams

Event apparel needs a useful intake: date, size range, sponsor or back-print requirements, quantity, and distribution plan. Bring those decisions to the quote so production can be scoped clearly.

Staff and organization apparel

For uniforms and recurring staff programs, include role variations, garment options, logo placement, and reorder needs. A clear initial plan makes the next run easier to manage.

Make the first quote useful

What to send before the Downtown project gets scheduled

A detailed request helps avoid choosing a print method or garment before the creative and operational needs are known.

  • Artwork file, logo, or a clear design direction
  • Garment preference, color, size range, and approximate quantity
  • Front, back, sleeve, or multiple print-location requirements
  • Event date, launch date, or other in-hands deadline
  • Any priority around hand feel, durability, color, or budget

Choose the finish after the brief

Use the print method that fits the Downtown order

Screen printing is a strong option for many repeatable apparel programs and event runs. Some projects benefit from DTF, embroidery, water-based ink, or discharge instead. The right choice depends on artwork, garment, coverage, quantity, and finish—not only the location.

Custom Screen Printing Downtown Colorado Springs FAQ

Short answers to the decisions that make a custom-apparel request easier to scope.

Can I request Downtown Colorado Springs retail merchandise or staff apparel?

Yes. Send the garment direction, artwork, quantity, print locations, and deadline through the quote form. ARK will use the details to recommend the appropriate production path.

What should I include in a Downtown event-apparel quote?

Include the event date, artwork, expected quantity, garment type, print locations, and any individual-name, size-distribution, or multiple-group needs.

Can ARK help choose between screen printing and DTF?

Yes. Artwork, quantity, garment, color count, and finish all influence the method. Include those details in the quote request so the recommendation matches the actual project.

Is ARK located in Downtown Colorado Springs?

ARK Screen Printing Studio is based in Colorado Springs and serves Downtown projects. The quote form is the best place to confirm the project’s production and fulfillment requirements.
