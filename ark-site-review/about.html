About ARK Screen Printing Studio

Personal attention and quality focus from a dedicated owner-operator since 2024

The ARK Story

ARK Screen Printing Studio was founded in 2024 by Alexander R. Kinnaman, bringing together his Master's-level education, artistic expertise, and commitment to the Colorado Springs community. What started as a passion for visual storytelling has evolved into a dedicated screen printing studio focused on delivering exceptional quality and personal service to every client.

Whether your needs are custom t-shirts for your local business, promotional items for your nonprofit, or unique designs for personal projects, ARK Screen Printing Studio has the expertise and dedication to bring your vision to life. Our owner-operated approach ensures that every project receives direct attention and care from concept to completion.
